SOCIAL STUDIES

Understanding Homelessness

Homelessness is the situation in which a person or family does not have a safe, stable, and permanent place to live. Some people stay in emergency shelters or temporary housing, while others sleep in cars, tents, or places not meant for living.

Who Can Experience Homelessness?

Families and Children

Families may lose their homes because of financial problems, high rent, or unexpected life events.

Young Adults

Some young people leave unsafe homes or struggle to afford housing and basic needs.

Older People

Older adults may face homelessness because of low income, health needs, or lack of family support.

People Who Lose Jobs

Unemployment can make it difficult to pay rent, bills, and other living costs.

Main Causes of Homelessness

1. High Housing Costs

Rent and house prices can be too expensive for people with low or limited incomes.

2. Unemployment

Losing a job or not finding stable employment can make it difficult to afford a home.

3. Poverty and Debt

Low wages, debt, and unexpected expenses can make families unable to pay for housing.

4. Family Difficulties

Family conflict, separation, or unsafe living situations can force people to leave their homes.

5. Natural Disasters

Floods, fires, hurricanes, and other disasters can destroy homes and displace families.

6. Health Challenges

Serious health problems and high medical costs can make it harder for some people to maintain housing.

Effects on People and Society

AreaPossible Effects
EducationChildren may miss school or struggle to study.
HealthPeople may have difficulty accessing healthcare and medicine.
SafetyPeople without safe housing may face dangerous conditions.
Family LifeFamilies may experience stress and separation.
EconomyEmergency services and community support systems face greater pressure.
CommunityHomelessness can affect community wellbeing and stability.

A 2024 UNITED STATES STATISTIC

771,480

People were counted as experiencing homelessness in the United States in January 2024, according to the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development.

This was an 18% increase from 2023. About 35% of the people counted were unsheltered.

These are historical 2024 figures, not live statistics.

POSSIBLE SOLUTIONS

How Can the USA Reduce Homelessness?

Homelessness needs more than one solution. Governments, charities, businesses, and communities can work together to help people find safe housing and become more independent.

Affordable Housing

Develop more affordable homes and help families who cannot afford high rental prices.

Emergency Shelters

Provide safe temporary places to sleep, clean facilities, and access to basic needs.

Employment and Training

Offer job training, career advice, and employment opportunities to help people earn stable incomes.

Healthcare Support

Connect people with medical care, mental health services, and necessary treatment.

Financial Assistance

Help eligible families with rent, utility bills, and other housing costs.

Preventing Homelessness

Identify families at risk early and provide support before they lose their homes.

UNITED ARAB EMIRATES

How Does the UAE Help People in Need?

The UAE has humanitarian organizations, charity campaigns, and government support programs. These efforts provide assistance to vulnerable people and families, both inside the country and internationally.

1. Emirates Red Crescent

Emirates Red Crescent is a humanitarian organization that provides assistance to people affected by poverty, disasters, and emergencies.

  • Distributes food and essential supplies.
  • Provides emergency and disaster relief.
  • Supports healthcare and medical assistance projects.
  • Contributes to housing and shelter projects.
  • Supports education and vulnerable communities.
  • Organizes charitable and volunteer activities.

How this could help the USA: Similar partnerships with trusted charities can help people find food, emergency supplies, temporary accommodation, and community support.

Official Emirates Red Crescent Website ↗

2. Dubai Temporary Housing Benefit

Dubai's Community Development Authority has a temporary housing benefit for eligible people who meet its program requirements.

  • Supports eligible people with temporary housing needs.
  • Can help families facing difficult housing situations.
  • Provides a form of support while people work toward more stable living arrangements.

How this could help the USA: Similar temporary housing assistance could give families a safe place while they search for permanent homes.

Dubai Community Development Authority ↗

3. UAE Government Housing Assistance

The UAE provides housing support programs for eligible UAE citizens through government housing services.

  • Support for building a home.
  • Assistance with purchasing suitable housing.
  • Support for completing or improving homes.
  • Housing loans or grants, depending on eligibility and program rules.

How this could help the USA: Housing assistance can help eligible families move toward long-term stability instead of depending only on temporary shelters.

UAE Ministry of Energy and Infrastructure ↗

4. Charity and Humanitarian Campaigns

Charitable campaigns in the UAE encourage people to support others through donations and volunteering.

  • Ramadan food distribution.
  • Clothing and winter donation campaigns.
  • School supplies for children in need.
  • Emergency relief collections.
  • Community volunteering.

How this could help the USA: Local campaigns can collect food, clothes, and essential supplies for people experiencing homelessness.

ISLAMIC STUDIES

What Does Islam Teach Us About Helping Others?

Islam teaches kindness, generosity, mercy, and responsibility toward people in need. Helping poor and homeless people is connected to the values of charity and social solidarity.

1. Zakat (الزكاة)

Zakat is the third pillar of Islam. It is an obligatory form of charity for Muslims who meet the required conditions and have qualifying wealth.

Importance of Zakat

  • Helps poor and needy people.
  • Reduces poverty and supports social fairness.
  • Encourages Muslims to share their wealth.
  • Strengthens relationships within society.
  • Reminds people that wealth comes with responsibility.

Connection to HomeHope USA: Zakat can support eligible people in need. Our website can explain the importance of charity and direct visitors to trusted organizations that handle donations responsibly.

2. Sadaqah (الصدقة)

Sadaqah is voluntary charity. It can be given in many ways, and it is not limited to money.

  • Donating money to trusted charities.
  • Giving food and clean water.
  • Donating clothes and useful items.
  • Helping someone find services or shelter.
  • Volunteering and giving time.
  • Sharing useful knowledge and advice.

Connection to HomeHope USA: Our website encourages visitors to discover volunteer opportunities and ways to support people in need.

3. Social Solidarity (التكافل الاجتماعي)

Social solidarity means that members of society support one another, especially during difficult times. Islam encourages people to care for their neighbors, families, orphans, poor people, and vulnerable groups.

Examples of Social Solidarity

  • Helping a family that cannot afford food.
  • Supporting people without safe housing.
  • Providing clothes and basic necessities.
  • Respecting the dignity of every person.
  • Working together to improve the community.

Connection to HomeHope USA: The website brings people, volunteers, and organizations together to create a supportive community.

INFORMATION AND COMMUNICATION TECHNOLOGY

How Does ICT Make a Difference?

Information and Communication Technology helps people access information, communicate, and find services more easily. Our website uses technology to connect people with organizations that may be able to help them.

HTML

HTML creates the structure of the website, including headings, paragraphs, sections, and links.

CSS

CSS controls the website's appearance, colours, layout, fonts, and responsive design.

JavaScript

JavaScript adds interactive features such as search, navigation, and information filters.

Online Maps

Maps can help visitors locate shelters, food banks, and other nearby services.

How Our Website Helps

  1. A visitor opens HomeHope USA.
  2. They choose the type of help they need.
  3. The website provides information and trusted links.
  4. The visitor contacts the relevant organization.
  5. The organization explains available services and eligibility.

Privacy and Safety

People experiencing homelessness may have sensitive personal circumstances. A responsible support website should protect visitors by avoiding unnecessary personal information, using trusted links, and clearly explaining how to contact service providers safely.
